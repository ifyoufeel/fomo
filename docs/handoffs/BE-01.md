## Decisions I made inside my area

- **pnpm 10.34.7, not 11 or 12.** pnpm 10 writes lockfile v9, which Vercel's build image detects and installs reliably. Revisit when Vercel documents pnpm 11+.
- **TypeScript `~6.0.3`, not 7.** TypeScript 7 (the native port) is out, but typescript-eslint 8.72 supports only TypeScript below 6.1. The tilde keeps us off 6.1 until that changes.
- **ESLint 10, not 9.** ESLint 9 is end-of-life and npm marks it deprecated. `eslint-config-next` pulls in `eslint-plugin-react`, `-import` and `-jsx-a11y`, whose peer ranges stop at 9. I checked that their rules run correctly on 10, and allowed 10 for those three plugins only in `peerDependencyRules`, so any new peer conflict still shows.
- **Lint is type-aware** (`recommendedTypeChecked`), because floating promises and `any` leaks are the bugs that hurt a ledger.
- **Production deploys from `main` are off** in each `vercel.json` until the owner approves going live. Phase 0 is "Nothing live", and production deployments need the owner's go-ahead. Pull-request previews are unaffected.
- **Markdown is excluded from Prettier.** Padded tables waste context in every CLAUDE.md and churn diffs in the document chats' files.
- **Dependabot is security-only for npm**, to meet the 48–72 hour rule without routine bump noise.
- **Not decided yet:** whether internal packages ship TypeScript source or compiled output. BE-02 decides this with the first shared library (`@fomo/db`). Risk to check then: Vercel's Hono builder may not transpile TypeScript inside workspace dependencies.
