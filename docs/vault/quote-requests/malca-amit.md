## Questions for Malca-Amit

Ask these on the call or in follow-ups.

1. Does the Taipei service centre opened in 2012 still operate? Can it collect from our hub and handle Taiwan export clearance?
2. Which liability exclusions apply in storage and in transit? In particular: war, confiscation, unexplained loss, and damage to fragile items (graded slabs, framed works).
3. Can you sign a monthly count that we publish? In what form, and what would it cost?
4. Can you photograph and inspect items on arrival, so that items bought abroad could go straight to Singapore when our Taiwan hub is closed?
5. What is the per-shipment limit on air transport from Taiwan, and do you offer war cover in transit?
6. Do you have a written emergency or business-continuity service for clients' goods in Taiwan?
7. How do you handle items with CITES-listed materials, such as exotic-leather straps?

## What not to say

- No investment language: no "returns", "yield", "appreciation" or "asset class". We sell collectibles.
- No claim about our legal status under virtual-asset rules. If they ask about tokens, say "each item has a digital ownership record; customers can hold it, resell it, or ask for delivery".
- No promise about values or volumes beyond the owner's figures.
