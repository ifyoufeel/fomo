## Questions for Brink's

Ask these on the call or in follow-ups.

1. Do you already store items for platforms that keep a digital ownership record per item? What reporting do they get from you?
2. Is the Taipei vault inside a bonded area or the taxed area? Do you run any bonded or free-trade-zone space in Taiwan?
3. Which liability exclusions apply in storage and in transit? In particular: war, confiscation, unexplained loss, and damage to fragile items (graded slabs, framed works).
4. Your transport contract limits liability to the declared value. Is there a per-shipment maximum, and can it be raised for an evacuation shipment?
5. Can you sign a monthly count that we publish? In what form, and what would it cost?
6. Can Brink's Singapore inspect and photograph items on arrival, so that items bought abroad could go straight to Singapore when our Taiwan hub is closed?
7. Do you offer war cover in transit from Taiwan, or should our own policy carry it?
8. Do you have a written emergency or business-continuity service for clients' goods in Taiwan?
9. How do you handle items with CITES-listed materials, such as exotic-leather straps?

## What not to say

- No investment language: no "returns", "yield", "appreciation" or "asset class". We sell collectibles.
- No claim about our legal status under virtual-asset rules. If they ask about tokens, say "each item has a digital ownership record; customers can hold it, resell it, or ask for delivery".
- Don't name other platforms that use Brink's. Ask in general terms (question 1).
- No promise about values or volumes beyond the owner's figures.
