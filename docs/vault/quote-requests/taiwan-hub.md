## What we ask for

- A lockable room of our own for receiving, filming, authenticating, photographing and recording items, with a rated safe or secured cabinets.
- Monitored security, access control and climate control.
- Answers on what work is allowed inside, on customs and tax, and on storage time limits.
- The ability to pack everything and hand it to a secure carrier within 48–72 hours in an emergency.

## Questions for the customs broker and counsel

These go to the customs broker, and to the Taiwan lawyer under CMP-02, not to the providers.

1. **Business tax on in-zone sales.** Is a sale to a buyer abroad of an item stored in the free trade zone or a bonded warehouse zero-rated, or outside Taiwan's business tax? This joins the brief's open export-tax question (`custody-comparison.md`, finding 6).
2. **Duty refund in the taxed area (option C).** If stock is imported into the taxed area and later exported to Singapore, can the import duty be refunded? On what conditions and within what time?
3. **Storage limits.** What are the storage limits and permitted operations under the current bonded-warehouse regulation, amended 20 July 2026?
4. **Exotic-leather straps.** Do they need CITES papers to enter and leave Taiwan under the Wildlife Conservation Act?
5. **Tariff codes.** Which codes apply to graded cards, sealed products, watches, bags, sneakers, toys and art, at import and at export?
