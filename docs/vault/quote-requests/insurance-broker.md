# Request to a Lloyd's broker: specie and fine-art cover

VLT-01 · draft · **not sent**. The owner sends it, after the checklist.

## How to use this

1. **Short calls with two brokers.** Gallagher first, then Howden or WTW (`custody-comparison.md`, section 6.5). Ask each how they would cover the Taiwan location (question 1 below) and what they know of collectibles.
2. **Appoint one** to approach the market. Two brokers asking the same underwriters block each other [G].
3. **Send that broker** the presentation below and ask for **indications** (non-binding terms) first. Bind only after the custodian is chosen, since the custodian's liability sets what our policy must add.

A customer who buys an item owns it; the item stays in our custody in an insured vault until the customer asks for delivery. We keep a digital ownership record for each item.

## What not to say

- No investment language. We sell collectibles; customers own items, not shares in them.
- No claim about our legal status under virtual-asset rules.
- Don't understate values to cut the premium. The insured value, the declared value and the buyback value all come from the same fair-market value (VLT-05). An under-declared value can void a carrier's liability [S].
