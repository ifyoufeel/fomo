## What we know from public sources

- **Storage** is free; **leaving** costs US$1.99 per item, plus US$4.99 for items vaulted under 90 days, plus shipping (US$5.99 per US shipment) [S].
- **Facility:** New Castle, Delaware, climate-controlled, with 24/7 security [S].
- **eBay:** PSA Vault replaced eBay's vault in 2024 [S].
- **Terms seen** [P]:
  - PSA may ask for information for sanctions checks, and restrict use if it gets no answer.
  - Items in an account with no activity for the period set by state law, typically three years, may be treated as abandoned.

## The message

> **Subject:** Business account enquiry: PSA Vault storage for a collectibles retailer
>
> Dear PSA team,
>
> We are `[owner: company legal name]`, a Taiwan company that sells graded trading cards and other collectibles from its own stock to customers in Taiwan and abroad. Each card a customer buys stays in an insured vault until the customer asks for delivery, and we keep a digital ownership record for every card, tied to its PSA certificate number.
>
> From `[owner: Phase 2 date]` we would like to buy PSA-graded cards from US sellers and keep them in the US, in a vault under our company's account. We expect about `[owner: number]` cards worth about US$`[owner: amount]` in the first year. Could you tell us:
>
> 1. Can a company outside the US open a PSA Vault account? Is there a business or dealer account, and on what terms?
> 2. Do your terms allow us to sell cards while they stay in your vault, recording the new owner on our own records, with the card shipped from your vault only when the owner asks for delivery?
> 3. Can we receive an item-level inventory of our account, by certificate number, as a daily download or by API?
> 4. Would PSA sign a monthly statement of the cards held in our account, which we may publish to our customers?
> 5. What insurance covers cards in the vault and in transit from it? What is the limit per account and per shipment, and on what value is a claim paid?
> 6. What are the fees for withdrawal and for international shipping to individual owners in Asia, Europe and elsewhere? Is there a business rate?
> 7. Can sellers ship cards directly into our vault account, and can cards be received from outside the US?
> 8. Is there an account minimum, a set-up process, or documents you need from us?
>
> Kind regards,
> `[owner: name, title, email, phone]`

## How to read the answer

| Answer | Then |

## What not to say

- No investment language: no "returns", "yield", "appreciation" or "asset class". We sell collectibles.
- No claim about our legal status under virtual-asset rules.
- Don't describe ownership records as fractions or shares: each card has one owner.
